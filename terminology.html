Terminology Dictionary of HVACR

冷凍空調術語辭典

整理冷凍、空調與能源領域常見術語，依英文詞條字母順序排列。每張卡片並列 IIR 與 ASHRAE 的英文解釋及中文翻譯。

Accumulator

中文：低壓循環桶 日文：アキュムレータ

IIR 定義

English

A vessel containing refrigerant (liquid or gas) and used for various purposes. See for instance: "receiver" or "low-pressure receiver".

中文翻譯

一種盛裝液態或氣態製冷劑、可用於不同用途的容器。例如：儲液器或低壓儲液器。

ASHRAE 定義

English

(1) apparatus to store cold by accumulation of ice on a coil. (2) pressure vessel connected to more than one circuit of a pneumatic system to obtain the average pressure of the connected circuits. (3) vessel for storing low pressure side liquid refrigerant, also known as a low pressure receiver, surge drum, surge header, or surge tank.

中文翻譯

(1) 透過在盤管上結冰來蓄存冷量的裝置。(2) 連接氣動系統中多個迴路的壓力容器，用來取得各連接迴路的平均壓力。(3) 用於儲存低壓側液態製冷劑的容器，也稱為低壓儲液器、緩衝桶、緩衝集管或緩衝槽。

Coefficient of performance (COP)

中文：性能係數 日文：動作係数、能力エネルギー比

IIR 定義

English

ratio of the refrigerating capacity to the power input.

中文翻譯

制冷能力與輸入功率之比。

ASHRAE 定義

English

(1) ratio of the rate of net heat output to the total energy input expressed in consistent units and under designated rating conditions. (2) ratio of the refrigerating capacity to the work absorbed by the compressor per unit time.

中文翻譯

(1) 在指定額定條件下，以一致單位表示的淨熱輸出速率與總能量輸入之比。(2) 制冷能力與壓縮機單位時間所吸收功之比。

Evaporator

中文：蒸發器 日文：蒸発器

IIR 定義

English

That part of a refrigeration system in which the refrigerant evaporates by absorbing heat from the contacting fluid.

中文翻譯

冷凍系統中，製冷劑從接觸流體吸收熱量並蒸發的部分。

ASHRAE 定義

English

heat exchanger, usually of the shell-and-tube type, over which liquid refrigerant is dripped or sprayed and evaporated.

中文翻譯

一種熱交換器，通常為殼管式；液態製冷劑滴淋或噴灑於其上並蒸發。

Face area

中文：迎風面積 日文：正面面積

IIR 定義

English

In a heat exchanger comprising a bundle of tubes, the cross-sectional area that is effectively traversed by the external fluid just before it passes through the tube bundle.

中文翻譯

在由管束構成的熱交換器中，外部流體通過管束前實際流經的截面積。

ASHRAE 定義

English

in a heat exchanger with a bundle of tubes, the section that is effectively traversed by the external fluid just before it passes through the tube bundle. The area measured is the lesser area of the two heat-exchanger surfaces.

中文翻譯

在具有管束的熱交換器中，外部流體通過管束前實際流經的截面。所測量的面積是兩個熱交換器表面中較小者。

Heat

中文：熱 日文：熱

IIR 定義

English

A form of energy which is exchanged between a system and its environment or between two systems subject to a temperature difference existing between them.

中文翻譯

一種能量形式，當系統與其環境之間或兩個系統之間存在溫差時，能量會在彼此之間交換。

ASHRAE 定義

English

(1) energy that is transferred in the direction of lower temperature. (2) form of energy that is exchanged between a system and its environment or between parts of the system induced by temperature difference existing between them.

中文翻譯

(1) 朝較低溫度方向傳遞的能量。(2) 因系統與其環境之間，或系統各部分之間存在溫差而交換的能量形式。

Receiver

中文：儲液器 日文：レシーバ、受液器

IIR 定義

English

A vessel, located on the high-pressure side of a refrigerating system, designed to ensure the availability of adequate liquid refrigerant for proper functioning of the system and to store the liquid refrigerant when the system is pumped down.

中文翻譯

設置於冷凍系統高壓側的容器，用以確保系統正常運作時有足夠的液態製冷劑，並在系統抽空收液時儲存液態製冷劑。

ASHRAE 定義

English

a vessel in the refrigerating system designed to ensure the availability of adequate liquid refrigerant for proper functioning of the system and to store the liquid refrigerant when the system is pumped down

中文翻譯

冷凍系統中的容器，用以確保系統正常運作時有足夠的液態製冷劑，並在系統抽空收液時儲存液態製冷劑。

Refrigerant

中文：冷媒、製冷劑 日文：冷媒

IIR 定義

English

Working fluid in a refrigerating system absorbing heat at low pressure/temperature and rejecting it at a higher pressure/temperature.

中文翻譯

冷凍系統中的工作流體，在低壓／低溫下吸收熱量，並在較高壓力／溫度下排出熱量。

ASHRAE 定義

English

the fluid used for heat transfer in a refrigerating system, which absorbs heat at a low temperature and a low pressure of the fluid and rejects it at a higher temperature and a higher pressure of the fluid, usually involving changes of the state of the fluid.

中文翻譯

用於冷凍系統中傳熱的流體，在流體處於低溫、低壓時吸收熱量，並在較高溫度、較高壓力下排出熱量，通常伴隨流體狀態變化。
